FADE IN:

INT. OFFICE BUILDING - DAY

JAKE (30s, disheveled suit) sits at a cluttered desk in a high-rise office. He stares at financial reports, rubbing his temples. Through the window, the city sprawls below.

EXT. BUSY STREET MARKET - DAY

MIRA (20s, sharp eyes) weaves through a crowded outdoor marketplace. Vendors shout prices. She stops at a spice stall, examining a small package passed to her under the counter.

INT. COFFEE SHOP - NIGHT

Jake and Mira sit across from each other in a dimly lit corner booth. Rain streaks the windows. Two cups of coffee sit untouched between them.

                    MIRA
          You said it was just paperwork.

                    JAKE
          It was. Until it wasn't.

EXT. ABANDONED WAREHOUSE DISTRICT - NIGHT

Jake approaches crumbling industrial buildings alone. Chain-link fences surround the property. Graffiti covers every surface. A single light flickers inside one building.

EXT. RIVERBRIDGE - DUSK

Mira stands on an old stone bridge spanning a wide river. The sunset paints the water orange and pink. She holds an envelope, staring at the current below.

INT. HOTEL ROOM - NIGHT

A cramped room with cheap furniture. Jake paces while Mira sits on the bed reviewing documents. A single lamp casts harsh shadows.

                    JAKE
          We need to get out of the city.

                    MIRA
          And go where?

EXT. MOUNTAIN TRAIL - DAWN

Mira hikes along a narrow trail through dense forest. Mist clings to the trees. Birds call in the distance. She checks a hand-drawn map.

INT. POLICE STATION - DAY

A sterile office with fluorescent lights. Jake reviews documents at a metal desk. A DETECTIVE (50s) watches him from across the room.

                    DETECTIVE
          You've got two hours, Mr. Cole.

FADE OUT.
